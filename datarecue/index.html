DataRescue

Recover deleted files, rebuild lost partitions, and carve data from raw disks and disk images — a local, read-only recovery tool for Windows.

What it does

DataRescue helps individuals and small businesses recover data lost to accidental deletion, formatting, or partition-table damage. It scans FAT/exFAT/NTFS/Ext4 volumes, rebuilds lost or corrupted partition tables, and carves files out of raw disk images whose file-system metadata is gone.

Deleted-file recovery
Scan a volume or whole physical disk and restore files that the file system no longer indexes.
Lost partition finder
Detect and rebuild partition tables that were lost or corrupted.
Signature carving
Recover JPEG, PDF, Office and other known file types from raw images when metadata is gone.
Local & private
All scanning happens on your machine. No file contents are uploaded anywhere.

Safety and privacy by design

  • Read-only on disks. The recovery engine opens disks for reading only — it never writes to the disk it scans.
  • Least privilege. The main app runs as a standard user. Only when you start a physical-disk scan does it launch a separate helper process with elevation (one UAC prompt), because reading raw sectors of \\.\PhysicalDriveN requires an administrator token.
  • Declining UAC is safe. If you decline the prompt, the app falls back to scanning disk-image files — nothing breaks.
  • No telemetry of your data. File contents and recovered data never leave your computer.
