2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Our Apps do not require an account and do not ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any other directly identifying information. If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use it only to respond to you.

2.2 Information stored on your device

App settings, game progress, high scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2.3 Information collected automatically by third-party services

The Apps include advertising and, where applicable, in-app billing components provided by Google. These third parties may collect information as described in Section 4.

2.4 Permissions

Some Apps request device features strictly to deliver their core functionality, for example the camera flash/torch (flashlight and strobe apps) or local storage (saving images you create). Such features are used only on your device and only when you actively use them. We do not collect or upload the content you create with them.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate the Apps and remember your settings and progress on your device.
  • To display advertising that keeps the Apps free, through Google AdMob.
  • To process in-app purchases, if an App offers them, through Google Play Billing.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To maintain the security, stability, and performance of the Apps, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for purposes unrelated to those described above.
